Celebration Timeline

Flow of Events

Two days of vibrant traditions, joyous music, and sacred vows in the royal city of Udaipur.

Day 1 • Morning Sat 26 Dec

Haldi

"Turmeric, marigolds, and a little chaos"

Sun-drenched laughter, floral showers, and drenching the bride and groom in auspicious turmeric love.

Time: 11:00 AM – 02:00 PM
Venue: Poolside Courtyard
Attire: Sun-kissed Yellows & Florals
Day 1 • Afternoon Sat 26 Dec

Bhat & Mayra

"The maternal family arrives with blessings and gifts"

Welcoming the maternal grandparents, uncles, and aunts with traditional dhol, sweets, and ceremonial blessings.

Time: 04:30 PM – 06:30 PM
Venue: The Heritage Terrace
Attire: Traditional Festive Pastels
Day 1 • Night Sat 26 Dec

Sangeet & Cocktails

"Two families, one dance floor"

Family dance face-offs, electrifying Bollywood beats, craft cocktails, and celebration under the stars.

Time: 07:30 PM Onwards
Venue: Royal Grand Ballroom
Attire: Shimmering Glamour & Indo-Western
Day 1 • Late Night Sat 26 Dec

Ghudchadi & Baraat

"The groom rides out with dhol and celebration"

Archit’s royal procession with live brass band, Punjabi dhols, and dancing family marching to the mandap.

Time: 10:30 PM Onwards
Venue: Main Palace Courtyard
Attire: Royal Sherwanis & Bandhgalas
Auspicious Muhurat Sun 27 Dec

Phere (The Holy Vows)

"Seven vows around the sacred fire"

Vedic mantras, sindoor daan, garland exchange (Varmala), and stepping together into an eternal bond of love.

Muhurat: 01:00 AM (Early hours of Dec 27)
Venue: Sunset Lakeside Mandap
Attire: Traditional Indian Royal / Ethnic Finery
Day 2 • Evening Sun 27 Dec

The Grand Reception

"Dinner, toasts, and a celebration of forever"

A grand evening banquet dinner, celebratory toasts, live music, and heartfelt congratulations to the newlyweds.

Time: 07:30 PM Late into the Night
Venue: The Palace Pavilion & Lawns
Attire: Black Tie / Formal Evening Royalty

Getting Here

By Air
Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) is ~45 minutes drive from the venue with direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru.
By Rail
Udaipur City Railway Station (UDZ) is well connected and only 20 minutes from the resort.
Pickups & Shuttles
Let the family know your arrival details in advance, and complimentary shuttle transfers will be coordinated.

Where to Stay

Resort & Guest Rooms
Comfortable guest accommodations are arranged for all out-of-town guests. Room allocation details will be shared upon arrival.
Family Stay Coordinators
For room queries and early check-in assistance, please contact the hospitality desk.
Valet Parking
Complimentary valet parking is available at the resort entrance throughout all functions.

Good to Know

Late-December Weather
Udaipur in late December enjoys pleasant, sunny afternoons (22°C) and cozy chilly evenings (10°C). Bringing a warm shawl, jacket, or bandhgala is recommended.
Getting Around
All celebrations take place within the resort property, so no commute is required between events.
